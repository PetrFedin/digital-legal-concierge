# Digital Legal Concierge — Evidence-to-Outcome Institutional Execution
Status: PROPOSED / documentation-only; 2026-10-11.
Companion to [Integration Master Plan](DIGITAL_LEGAL_CONCIERGE_INTEGRATION_MASTER_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) and [Canonical MVP Spec](CANONICAL_MVP_SPEC_2026-10-03.md).

## P0 — dependable case resolution
Clear next-action case workspace: current stage, required evidence, filing/deadline checklist, responsible party and user-readable reason. Keep CaseService, encryption, retention, payment and audit as sole authorities; never duplicate case status.

## Trust and integrations
P0: Original immutable -> malware admission -> derived OCR/structured citation -> human-verified fact -> calculator snapshot -> reviewed document -> signed receipt -> action log. Preserve page-level provenance and disputed facts.
P1: Policy-sensitive deadline and penalty computation with explicit as-of legislation/version, jurisdiction, assumptions and reproducible tests; no automated promise of legal outcome.
P1: Lawyer/partner verification program: professional eligibility evidence, status/expiry/revocation, permitted service scope, human acceptance and transparent referral fee accounting.
P1: E-sign, filing and delivery adapters only after verifying legal applicability, user authorization, delivery confirmations and provider terms. Never claim filing occurred from a locally generated PDF.
P2: Enterprise/white-label housing developer, insurer and lawyer portals with tenant isolation and conflict-of-interest controls; independent document verifier with access-scoped disclosure.
P2: Consent-based anonymized practice intelligence; redact personal data before any aggregate analytics and apply minimum cohort thresholds.

## Workload and acceptance
Existing case assignment -> legal reviewer, client, document specialist, administrator and external partner responsibility views (no shadow assignments). Security tests: tenant isolation, object authorization, encryption/rotation, retention and legal hold, evidence mutation/replay, accessibility, recovery and independent human legal review.
